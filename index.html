    About Royal Ceramic Industries

    Three decades of porcelain,
    engineered to endure

    Founded in Thangadh — India's historic ceramic town — Royal Ceramic Industries has grown from a family workshop into a trusted manufacturer of electrical insulation components. We support utilities, EPC contractors and industrial buyers with precision manufacturing, disciplined quality control and honest delivery commitments.

    Our Mission

    Deliver durable ceramic solutions that meet demanding electrical standards and keep infrastructure running.

    Our Vision

    Become the benchmark for premium electrical ceramics in India through innovation and reliability.
